# Product

register: product

## What this is
Sheet Music Reader turns psalm charts (PDFs) into things a singer can practise with: a score player,
slowed practice files, and a karaoke trainer that highlights each note above the word being sung while
background music plays.

## Who uses it
Parish cantors and choir singers, many of whom do not play piano. They practise at home at a table or
music stand, on a laptop or a phone, in ordinary room light, and are used to reading printed music.

## What the interface must do
- Read like printed music: white page, dark ink. One highlighter colour marks "sing this now".
- Lyrics are the largest text. The note and its word are always shown together.
- Every control works with a thumb on a 390 px wide phone. No sideways scrolling.
- Plain words on controls ("Repeat this line", "Count me in"), no music-software jargon.
- Works offline from a single HTML file.

## Not wanted
Dark neon "studio" styling on the trainer, decorative gradients or glass, emoji as icons.
